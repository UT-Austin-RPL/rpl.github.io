The [Robot Perception and Learning (RPL) Lab]({{ '/' | relative_url }}) at the University of Texas at Austin has multiple openings for postdoc positions in the areas of **Robot Learning** and **Embodied AI**. The anticipated start date is in the range of **October 1st, 2023** to **March 31st, 2024**. This position will remain open until filled. Positions are for a one-year term, with the possibility of renewal for additional years.

---

#### Essential Qualifications

-	Ph.D. earned within the last three years in the fields of Artificial Intelligence and/or Robotics;
- Excellent technical abilities and programming skills;
-	Productive, self-driven, and collaborative scholar with a strong publication track record;
-	Strong communication skills and ability to convey research ideas to a broad audience.

#### Preferred Qualifications

-	Knowledge of robotics simulation and foundation models;
-	Experience working on physical robot hardware;
- Experience working with large-scale machine learning systems;
- Experience mentoring students and/or leading project teams of 3+ researchers.


#### Required Materials

Please contact Yuke Zhu ([yukez@cs.utexas.edu](mailto:yukez@cs.utexas.edu)) with the following materials if interested:

1. Cover letter (up to two pages),
2. Curriculum vitae,
3. Names and contact information of 3-5 references,
4. Electronic copies of two most representative publications.
